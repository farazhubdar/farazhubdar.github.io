Stop 01 · Demand

Every project starts as a demand.

A kitchen begins as a drawing and a deadline. My job is to turn it into the right material, from the right supplier, on the right day.

I joined The Art Lines, a 65-person kitchen and interior manufacturer, as Warehouse Manager in 2020 and was promoted to Procurement Manager in 2023. Today I run the full cycle for seven production divisions: reading demand from the project pipeline, sourcing, purchasing, warehousing and routing daily deliveries.

Six years as a buyer taught me how buyers think. I'm now using that on the other side of the table, as co-founder of Idrees Traders & Corporation, and while I finish a BS specialising in Brand Management & Marketing Communications.
