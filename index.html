About

Buying smart, building brands.

I joined The Art Lines, a 65-person kitchen and interior manufacturer, as Warehouse Manager in 2020 and was promoted to Procurement Manager in 2023. Today I run the full supply chain cycle — demand analysis, sourcing, purchasing, warehousing and daily delivery routing — for seven production divisions.

Alongside it, I co-founded Idrees Traders & Corporation, an architectural hardware brand, and I'm completing a BS in Public Administration specialising in Brand Management & Marketing Communications. Six years as a buyer taught me how buyers think; now I use that to build and sell brands.
